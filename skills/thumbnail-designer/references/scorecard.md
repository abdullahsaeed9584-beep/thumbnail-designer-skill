# Scorecard

Score every build before delivery, and every thumbnail you critique. 10 axes × 0-4. **Ship at ≥32/40 with no axis at 0.**

| # | Axis | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Focal clarity** — one unmistakable subject | eye has nowhere to land | two competing elements | instant single focal point, holds under blur |
| 2 | **Hierarchy & flow** — subject → text → detail | random | readable but flat | deliberate Z-path, clear 1st/2nd/3rd |
| 3 | **Legibility at 168 px** | unreadable | squint-readable | effortless |
| 4 | **Grayscale contrast** | collapses | muddy in places | full separation on luminance alone |
| 5 | **Text discipline** — ≤5 words, no title repeat, ≤2 fonts | wall of text | 6-8 words or 3 fonts | 2-4 words, 1 accent keyword |
| 6 | **Emotion / curiosity gap** | none | generic smile | specific feeling + an open question |
| 7 | **Light & depth** — rim light, contact shadow, 3 layers, DOF | flat, pasted | shadow but no rim / no depth | grounded, separated, cinematic |
| 8 | **Colour** — 2-3 + accent, controlled saturation, true skin | chaos or over-sat | serviceable | disciplined, high separation, natural skin |
| 9 | **Craft & finish** — edges, alignment, grain, sharpness, export | halos, artefacts | minor edge issues | clean edges, cohesive grain, spec-perfect export |
| 10 | **Honesty & title fit** | misleading | vague stretch | true, pays off, complements the title |

Axis 10 is a **hard gate**: a 0 is a block regardless of total.

## Reporting a critique
1. The table with scores and one line of evidence each.
2. **Top 3 fixes, ordered by CTR impact** — concrete ("headline from 9 words to 3; move it to upper-left off the face"), never vague.
3. What to keep — don't let a rebuild destroy what already works.
4. Offer a revised version.
Never predict a CTR number or claim which variant will win. Name the hypothesis instead.
